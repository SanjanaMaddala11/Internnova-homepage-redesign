06 Good to know

Questions?
Start here.

Need a detail before you choose your track? These quick answers can help you get oriented.

Visit the official website ↗
Do I need prior coding experience?+

Requirements depend on the selected track. Review the program details and choose a starting point that matches your current skills.

What will I work on during a track?+

Programs may include learning resources, practical tasks, and project work. Check the specific track page for its current curriculum and deliverables.

How long does a program take?+

The current website describes standard learning journeys of around four to six weeks, though the exact schedule can vary by program and cohort.

Will I receive a certificate?+

Certificate eligibility and verification depend on the program's completion requirements. Read the terms for your selected program before enrolling.

Are all internships paid?+

Not necessarily. The official terms state that standard internships are unpaid unless compensation is explicitly mentioned. Confirm all fees and terms before applying.

Where can I find the official program details?+

Use the official InternNova website to review current internship listings, eligibility, dates, and application details.
